HOW IT WORKS

Three moves. Zero damage.

KillSwitch sits between your agent and the shell. Every command is scored before it executes — in 12ms.

01

Intercept

The gateway taps every terminal call, API request and SQL statement. Nothing executes until the engine scores it.

→ gateway intercepts exec()

02

Score & steer

A risk model blends blast radius, auth, and history. Low risk auto-approves. High risk pauses for your one-tap decision.

risk = f(blast, auth, history)

03

Kill or release

Approve in one tap — or terminate the session instantly. Every decision is signed and replayable for audit.
